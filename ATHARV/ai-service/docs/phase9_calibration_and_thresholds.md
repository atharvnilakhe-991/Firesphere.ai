## 1. Executive Summary & Conceptual Distinction

A foundational principle of scientific integrity in the FireSphere AI service is the strict distinction between **operational confidence thresholding** and **statistical probability calibration**:

1. **Operational Confidence Thresholding ($\tau$):**  
   Selecting a threshold $\tau \in [0.0, 1.0]$ designates an operating point on the empirical precision-recall curve. Lowering $\tau$ increases recall at the cost of precision; raising $\tau$ increases precision at the cost of recall. A threshold choice does **NOT** alter the underlying distribution of model activations and does **NOT** fix poor model representations.
2. **Statistical Probability Calibration:**  
   A model is calibrated if and only if a prediction score $p \in [0.0, 1.0]$ matches the empirical probability of correct classification:
   $$P(\hat{Y} = Y \mid \hat{P} = p) = p$$
   Calibration requires a designated, held-out validation dataset, reliability diagrams, Expected Calibration Error (ECE) computation, and post-hoc transformation (e.g., Platt Scaling, Temperature Scaling, or Isotonic Regression).

## 3. Operational Threshold Selection Methodology

Threshold selection was executed systematically on the audited Phase 8.8 validation split (928 images, 1,443 annotations) across $\tau \in [0.05, 0.50]$:

| Threshold ($\tau$) | Precision | Recall | F1-Score | mAP@50 | Extinguisher AP50 | Exit Sign AP50 | Empirical Assessment |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **0.05** | 0.0682 | 0.1988 | 0.1004 | 0.0267 | 0.0781 | 0.0282 | Excessive false positive noise; precision collapses to 6.8%. |
| **0.10** | 0.1299 | 0.1547 | 0.1396 | 0.0418 | 0.1168 | 0.0505 | Transition zone; high background clutter. |
| **0.15 (Selected)** | **0.1967** | **0.1248** | **0.1495** | **0.0506** | 0.1401 | **0.0624** | **Global peak in validation F1-score and validation mAP@50.** |
| **0.20** | 0.2343 | 0.0980 | 0.1354 | 0.0479 | **0.1404** | 0.0512 | Recall degrades below 10%. |
| **0.30** | 0.3219 | 0.0678 | 0.1100 | 0.0462 | 0.1381 | 0.0467 | Overly conservative; misses >93% of valid objects. |
| **0.50** | 0.4040 | 0.0288 | 0.0534 | 0.0275 | 0.0832 | 0.0270 | Impractical operational recall (2.8%). |

### Strict Evaluation Governance Invariants:
1. **Validation Data Only:** The threshold $\tau = 0.15$ was selected solely using validation data.
2. **Zero External Test Tuning:** The untouched Phase 8.3 test set (522 images) was **NEVER** used to tune, search, or bias operational thresholds.
3. **No Arbitrary Class-Specific Thresholds:** Single uniform threshold $\tau = 0.15$ is applied across all classes to prevent unprincipled metric gaming.
4. **Preservation of Low-Confidence Detections:** Low-confidence detections ($0.05 \le \tau < 0.15$) are retained in debug/audit logs for controlled failure analysis.
